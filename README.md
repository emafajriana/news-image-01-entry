# The World Today — Friday 13 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-01** ("Sum up 13 November's
world news in one image").

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the bundled
picture against the competition limits (whole PNG or JPEG, at most
1 MiB, at most 1024x1024) and copies it byte for byte to
`entry.png` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set,
otherwise `/output`).

The picture: a newspaper front page, "THE WORLD TODAY — FRIDAY
13 NOVEMBER 2026 — THE DAY IN ONE PICTURE", with six illustrated
panels — Nvidia chief Jensen Huang resigns (shares plunge 16%);
tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz as Iran says a US radar in
Bahrain was destroyed; Europe joins the Ukraine peace talks;
Microsoft outage goes global; Gaza hospital strike in Khan Younis
kills six; Brooklyn building collapse toll reaches eight — plus an
"Also Today" strip with eight more of the day's stories.
