# The World Today — Friday 13 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-01** ("Sum up 13 November's
world news in one image").

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the bundled
picture against the competition limits (whole PNG or JPEG, at most
1 MiB, at most 1024x1024) and copies it byte for byte to
`entry.png` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set,
otherwise `/output`).

The picture: a newspaper front page, "THE WORLD TODAY — FRIDAY
13 NOVEMBER 2026 — THE DAY IN ONE PICTURE", with eight illustrated
panels — Nvidia chief Jensen Huang resigns (CFO Colette Kress named
interim CEO; shares plunge 16%); tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz
as Iran says a US radar in Bahrain was destroyed; Europe joins the
Ukraine peace talks; Microsoft outage goes global; Gaza hospital
strike in Khan Younis kills six; Brooklyn building collapse toll
reaches eight; Pakistan raids near the Afghan border kill 74
militants; Houthis claim strikes on Saudi Arabia's East-West oil
pipeline and Yanbu (Brent crude jumps 3.4%) — plus an "Also Today"
strip with eleven more of the day's stories: Taiwan details China's
Pacific drills; the IEA forecasts an oil surplus; the US seizes
$1.2bn from the Binance hack; Pope Leo meets survivors in Peru;
England beat Croatia; a Democrat wins the Kansas governorship;
Storm Charles hits Portugal; Trump says no Iran deal is needed;
Trump threatens to double tariffs on Mexico over migrant caravans;
a Moscow car bomb wounds a former Ukrainian prime minister's aide;
and the US and Mexico await a ruling on their measles elimination
status. Nineteen stories in total.
